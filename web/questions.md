# Questionnaire questions

Source of truth in code: `src/components/questionnaire/steps.ts`

---

## Part 1 — About you

### Gender (step 0)
- `gender` — I am a (man / woman)

### Basic information (step 1)
- `age` — Age
- `country` — Country
- `city` — City
- `height` — Height (cm)
- `weight` — Weight (kg)
- `languagesSpoken` — Languages you speak

### Religious practice (step 2)
- `prayerFrequency` — Do you perform the five daily prayers?
- `wearsHijab` — Do you wear hijab? *(women only)*

### Education (step 3)
- `education` — Education Level

### Employment (step 4)
- `occupation` — Employment Status
- `marriageWorkPreference` — After marriage, what is your preference? *(women only)*

### Marriage & family (step 5)
- `maritalStatus` — What is your marital status?

### Lifestyle (step 6)
- `substanceUse` — Do you use any substances (smoking, drugs, etc.)?
- `substanceDetails` — Please describe what you use *(if Yes)*

### About you (step 7)
- `marriageTimeline` — Marriage Timeline
- `qualities` — Choose up to 10 qualities that describe you
- `hobbies` — Choose your hobbies

---

## Part 2 — Partner preferences (step 8)

- `pref_partnerHijabLevel` — Hijab / niqab preference for your spouse *(men only)*
- `pref_minAge` — Preferred Age *(choose one number)*
- `pref_minHeight` — Preferred Height *(choose one number)*
- `pref_minWeight` — Preferred Weight (kg) *(choose one number)*
- `pref_preferredCountries` — Preferred Countries
- `pref_educationLevel` — Preferred Education

## Contact (step 9)

- `name` — Full name
- `phone` — Phone number
