Hel Calafkaaga helps serious Muslim men and women explore marriage compatibility with dignity and privacy.

Hel Calafkaaga is for adults 18 and older. We do not guarantee matches. We do not claim end-to-end encryption, background checks, or identity verification.

Support: support@helcalafkaaga.com  
Privacy Policy and Terms are available in the app and on our website.
