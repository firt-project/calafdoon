# Legal & policy review list (engineering checklist)

**Not legal advice.** Route to qualified counsel before public launch.

- [ ] Privacy Policy matches actual processors (Postgres, Redis, S3, Stripe, email, hosting)
- [ ] Terms of Service (UGC, bans, payments, liability)
- [ ] Community Guidelines
- [ ] Age eligibility (18+) — server enforces self-reported age ≥ 18; DOB verification still absent
- [ ] User-generated content moderation obligations
- [ ] Reporting / blocking / safety tooling adequacy
- [ ] Account deletion timelines vs legal retention (payments, abuse evidence)
- [ ] Data export / access rights (region-dependent)
- [ ] Payment & refund terms
- [ ] Subscription cancellation (if subscriptions introduced)
- [ ] Marketing / email / push consent
- [ ] Cross-border transfers
- [ ] Relationship-app safety disclosures
- [ ] Store data-safety / nutrition labels accuracy
- [ ] Somali + English consumer clarity
